# Abstract Coined Global Brand Names for a Saudi-born Cloud ERP (vetting as of Nov 2026)

Method: I made up 39 names from Latin/Greek roots for wholeness, balance, measure and rules (telos, isos, metron, kanon, integer/intera, orbis, libra, omni, kalos). Before searching, I screened each one against the hard rules: no P or V, no C/G/X (their Latin pronunciation is ambiguous and g is not native to Arabic), 4–7 letters, 2–3 syllables, and no close resemblance to the excluded list. Each surviving name then got at least one exact-match web search ("<name>" software OR app OR company/fintech), and the strongest names got a second pass covering App Store, Google Play and Crunchbase, startup/fintech terms, and "<name>.com".

**Tool limits (important):** This session's network sandbox blocked direct fetches of the candidate .com sites (`getaddrinfo ENOTFOUND` for telano.com, isona.com, metrona.com, kanona.com, intara.com, orbeo.com, kalmira.com, darona.com). So "no evidence of active .com use" means only that **no indexed pages came up in search**. It does not confirm the domain is unregistered. I did not search the WIPO Global Brand Database, USPTO, EUIPO or the Saudi IP Authority (SAIP).

## 1. Which coined names survive vetting (shortlist of 10 + 1 conditional)

### Takeaway
Ten names had no exact-match software, SaaS, fintech or app use in any search. The strongest are **Intara, Telano, Orbeo and Kalmira**: each has a clear concept, one obvious spelling, easy Arabic script and no negative meaning in the six languages. Metrona, Kanona, Libena, Omnado, Isona and Kalisa are usable, each with a minor caveat. Darona is conditional.

### Cited Findings

**1. Intara — إنتارا** (in-TAA-ra, 3 syllables, 6 letters)
- Evokes: Latin *integer* and Italian *intera*, "whole/entire" (Spanish *entera* is the same idea). It signals integration of all modules.
- Pronunciation: AR إنتارا is clean, with every letter native (ت ر ن). EN "in-TAR-a" is the single natural reading.
- Negative meaning: none found in AR/EN/UR/TR/FR/ES. To Arabic ears it echoes the hero-poet عنترة (Antara), which is a positive association. Urdu *antar* means "difference" (neutral).
- Conflicts: the exact-name search found no software, app or company called Intara. The nearest are Itara IT Solutions (India), the INTRA iOS app, the Inara iOS app, Intakra and IntrApp, all with different spellings — [search results incl. Itara](https://m.indiamart.com/itara-it-solution), [INTRA app](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/intra/id1344140120), [Intakra](https://www.getapp.com/all-software/a/intakra/). App Store, Google Play and Crunchbase searches found no Intara listing, only INARA, Intaker, Intentra and similar — [Google Play dev INARA](https://play.google.com/store/apps/developer?id=INARA), [Intentra](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.intentra.app). A "intara.com" search returned only Intira Corporation, a hosting company that went bankrupt in 2001 — [Dealroom](https://dealroom.co/companies/intira-corporation/).
- .com: no indexed pages. A direct fetch was blocked by the sandbox.

**2. Telano — تيلانو** (te-LAA-no, 3 syllables, 6 letters)
- Evokes: Greek *telos*, "completion / end-goal", i.e. complete and finished.
- Pronunciation: AR تيلانو is clean. EN "teh-LAH-no" is unambiguous.
- Negative meaning: none. ES *telón* means "curtain" and *tela* means "fabric", both neutral. TR *tel* means "wire" and AR تل means "hill", both neutral.
- Conflicts: no exact match. The nearest are Telana, an AI software development firm, plus Teleons Software and TeliApp — [Telana](https://telana.com/?p=9), [Teleons](https://clutch.co/profile/teleons-software). A "telano.com" search returned only Telono, a Geneva UX agency at telono.com — [ZoomInfo Telono](https://www.zoominfo.com/c/telono-sa/350758514). A Crunchbase/startup search found no Telano — [search showed only Telo](https://www.startuphub.ai/startups/telo).
- Note: Teleo, the Greek-root cousin, is taken (see rejects).

**3. Orbeo — أوربيو** (OR-be-o, 3 syllables, 5 letters)
- Evokes: Latin *orbis* (circle, the whole world) and ES *orbe* (globe), i.e. global, all-encompassing and closed-loop.
- Pronunciation: AR أوربيو is clean, though the e-o vowel run must be voiced as "or-bee-yo". EN "OR-bee-oh" is natural.
- Negative meaning: none found.
- Conflicts: no exact match. The nearest are Órbo (legal deadlines), Orbo Technologies (Dublin data security) and Orfeo Mobile — [Órbo](https://www.legaltechnologyhub.com/vendors/orbo/), [Orbo Technologies](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/orbo-technologies), [Orfeo Mobile](https://apps.apple.com/mx/app/orfeo-mobile/id1586807401). A "orbeo.com" search returned nothing.

**4. Kalmira — كالميرا** (kal-MEE-ra, 3 syllables, 7 letters)
- Evokes: Greek *kalos* ("good, beautiful") plus *mira* (ES/IT "look, view"), i.e. a clear and beautiful view of the business.
- Pronunciation: AR كالميرا is clean and reads as "كالأميرة" ("like a princess"), a pleasant association. EN "kal-MEER-a" is unambiguous.
- Negative meaning: none. TR *kal* means "stay" and UR *kal* means "tomorrow/yesterday", both neutral.
- Conflicts: no exact match. The nearest are Kalmly (AI communication assistant), Kalm Tech, KALMARS (Riga agency), Kalmia Ltd and Zenmira — [Kalmly](https://www.crunchbase.com/organization/kalmly-ltd), [KALMARS](https://clutch.co/profile/kalmars-0), [Zenmira](https://clutch.co/profile/zenmira). A "kalmira.com" search returned only WHOIS keyword pages for other names — [whoxy calmora](https://www.whoxy.com/keyword/calmora).
- Caveat: the "-mira" ending is fairly common in new coinages (e.g. Zenmira).

**5. Kanona — كانونا** (ka-NOO-na, 3 syllables, 6 letters)
- Evokes: Greek *kanon* ("rule, standard"). For Urdu and Arabic speakers it echoes قانون *qanun* ("law"), which fits compliance and ZATCA.
- Pronunciation: AR كانونا is clean. EN "ka-NO-na" is unambiguous.
- Negative meaning: none found. AR كانون is also a brazier or hearth and a Levantine month name, all neutral. FR *canon* is neutral or positive slang.
- Conflicts: no exact match. The nearest are KANNA (construction PM app), Kanova (retail app) and Kanusoft — [KANNA on Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/10006844/KANNA/), [Kanova](https://apps.apple.com/pl/app/kanova/id1537509200?l=pl), [Kanusoft](https://clutch.co/profile/kanusoft). A "kanona.com" search returned nothing.

**6. Metrona — ميترونا** (me-TRO-na, 3 syllables, 7 letters)
- Evokes: Greek *metron* ("measure"), i.e. measurement and accurate numbers.
- Pronunciation: AR ميترونا is clean. EN "meh-TRO-na" is unambiguous.
- Negative meaning: none. ES/IT *matrona* ("matron, midwife") is close but not negative.
- Conflicts: no software match in search. The nearest are Metron (vet imaging, payroll consulting), Metronome (billing infra) and Metlone (a business-management app with accounts, HR and inventory) — [Metron EponaTech](https://sourceforge.net/software/product/Metron-EponaTech/), [Metronome](https://www.getapp.ca/software/2056868/metronome), [Metlone](https://apps.apple.com/nz/app/metlone/id1468457567). Metlone is an adjacent ERP-like app, but the name is clearly different.
- Caveat: see Gaps on a possible German metering company of this name.

**7. Libena — ليبينا** (li-BEE-na, 3 syllables, 6 letters)
- Evokes: Latin *libra* ("balance scale"). Arabic speakers may also hear لَبِنة (*libna*, "a brick / building block"), a positive metaphor for a platform.
- Pronunciation: AR ليبينا is clean. EN "li-BEE-na" is natural.
- Negative meaning: none. It may faintly recall لبنة/labneh (food), which is harmless.
- Conflicts: no exact match. The nearest are Libeo (custom software), Libesent, Libanto and Libon — [Libeo](https://www.goodfirms.co/company/libeo), [Libanto](https://techbehemoths.com/company/libanto-inc), [Libon](https://app.dealroom.co/companies/libon/team).

**8. Omnado — أومنادو** (om-NAA-do, 3 syllables, 6 letters)
- Evokes: Latin *omni-* ("all"), i.e. everything in one platform.
- Pronunciation: AR أومنادو is clean. EN "om-NAH-doh" is unambiguous.
- Negative meaning: none found. The Spanish "-ado" ending sounds participle-like but the word means nothing.
- Conflicts: no exact match. The nearest are Omnicado (e-commerce integrations), Omnna (business database platform) and Omnis Studio — [Omnicado](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/omnicado), [Omnna](https://www.sourceforge.net/software/product/Omnna).
- Caveat: Omnicado is phonetically somewhat close and also does business integration.

**9. Isona — إيسونا** (ee-SO-na, 3 syllables, 5 letters)
- Evokes: Greek *isos* ("equal"), i.e. balance, as in balanced books.
- Pronunciation: AR إيسونا is clean. EN has two possible readings ("eye-SO-na" or "ee-ZO-na"), which weakens the "one obvious pronunciation" rule.
- Negative meaning: none found.
- Conflicts: no software, app or fintech by this name. The nearest are the ISOLN wealth app, ISONet and ISONAS (access-control hardware) — [ISOLN](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/isoln/id1473020059), [ISONAS on Crunchbase](https://www.crunchbase.com/organization/isonas).

**10. Kalisa — كاليسا** (ka-LEE-sa, 3 syllables, 6 letters)
- Evokes: *kal-* (Greek *kalos* "good", Latin *calculus*). The concept link is weaker than the others.
- Pronunciation: AR كاليسا is clean. EN mostly "ka-LEE-sa", with some risk of "ka-LEE-za".
- Negative meaning: none found.
- Conflicts: no exact match. The nearest are fintechs KalPay (e-wallet), Caliza (cross-border payments), Kisa and Callidus Pay — [KalPay](https://apps.apple.com/gm/app/id1453930573?l=en-gb), [Caliza](https://saasbrowser.com/saas/119649/caliza). Caliza is a phonetically similar payments fintech, which is a moderate risk in a payments context.

**Conditional: Darona — دارونا** (da-ROO-na)
- Evokes: Arabic دار ("home, house"), i.e. the business's home base. This makes it less abstract.
- Conflicts: no exact match. The nearest are DARON, a logistics operations platform in development, and Darlean, a business-management app — [DARON](https://sites.google.com/view/daronio/daron-home), [Darlean](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/darlean/id6737303586).
- Negative meaning: TR *dar* means "narrow/tight", mildly negative.

### Inferences
- Ranking by meaning fit, conflict distance and pronunciation: 1 Intara, 2 Telano, 3 Orbeo, 4 Kalmira, 5 Kanona, 6 Libena, 7 Metrona, 8 Omnado, 9 Isona, 10 Kalisa.
- Intara is the best fit for an integrated ERP: three of the six target languages (IT/ES-adjacent and Latin) read it as "whole", and it has a positive Arabic echo (Antara).
- All ten avoid the endings -ora, -ify, -ix, -ly and -io. Five end in "-na" or "-a", which is common but not as crowded as -ora.

### Gaps
- No live check of .com registration or use (sandbox blocked). WHOIS on each domain is needed before any decision.
- No trademark database searches (WIPO GBD, SAIP, USPTO, EUIPO) in classes 9, 35, 36 and 42. These are needed before final selection.
- **Metrona:** I recall, but did not verify in this session, a German metering and billing services firm possibly named "Metrona". This needs checking.
- **Isona:** I believe Isona is a municipality in Catalonia, Spain (neutral), but did not confirm it in this session.
- **Kalisa:** I believe it is a common Rwandan surname (neutral), but did not confirm it.
- The negative-meaning checks across six languages are my own linguistic screening; no native-speaker panel or slang database was consulted. A quick native check in Urdu, Turkish and Gulf Arabic slang is recommended for the final 3.

Twenty-two of the 33 searched names (plus 6 screened without search) were rejected. The main reason was an existing software, app or fintech company with the same name or one that sounds the same. A few were dictionary words or too close to the user's excluded list.

### Cited Findings (screened out before search, by rule)
- Kosma (Arabic/Turkish vulgar *kos*), Sika (Turkish vulgar *sik*), Luna-root names (Urdu vulgar *lun*), Ayra (Arabic vulgar *ayr*): excluded on negative meaning. No URL; this was a linguistic screen.
- Sumado (Spanish dictionary word, "added"): dictionary word.
- Taleo: the former Taleo talent-management SaaS (acquired by Oracle) — recalled from memory, not re-searched.

### Inferences
- The space of 3-syllable Latin-root coinages ending in -a is very crowded with small agencies and apps. Names at edit distance 1 from a SaaS (Talana, Ordaana, Izaro, Unolo, Kaltura) should be treated as conflicts, given the global scope.
- Five of the rejects collided specifically with business, ERP, HR or finance software (Talana, Ordaana, Izaro, SimorSoft, Sinao), which confirms how important the category-specific search was.

### Gaps
- A single exact-match search per rejected name was enough to reject it. I did not do deeper verification of whether each conflicting company is still active.

## 3. Recommended next steps for the shortlist

### Takeaway
Before committing, run WHOIS and trademark clearance on the top 4 (Intara, Telano, Orbeo, Kalmira), and get a native-speaker slang check in Gulf Arabic, Urdu and Turkish.

### Cited Findings
- No trademark or WHOIS data was retrieved in this session (the sandbox blocked domain hosts; trademark databases were not queried). See section 1 Gaps.

### Inferences
- If the .com is taken but parked, fallback forms like get<name>.com, <name>.app, <name>erp.com or <name>.sa suit a Saudi launch. Securing the .sa ccTLD early is prudent.
- Arabic logotype: all four top names start with simple letter forms (إ / ت / أ / ك) and contain no hamza ambiguity mid-word, which keeps the Arabic wordmark clean.

### Gaps
- Domain availability, trademark status and App Store / Google Play handle availability for the final names are still unverified.
