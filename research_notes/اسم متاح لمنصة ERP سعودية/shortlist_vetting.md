# Shortlist vetting: five Arabic brand names for a Saudi cloud ERP (as of 25 Nov 2026)

Method and limits, read first:
- About 14 web searches in Arabic and English, using the main spellings and the words "app", "software", "platform", "تطبيق", "منصة", "شركة" and "trademark". The search tool returns summarised snippets, not full app-store or registry pages.
- I could not open any page directly. Every WebFetch call failed with "host not allowed / ENOTFOUND", including apps.apple.com, ijmal.com, marqoom.com, raseen.com, thabt.com and damma.com. Direct `curl` to ijmal.com, ijmal.sa, ejmal.com, ijmaal.com, damma.com, dammah.com, damma.sa, marqoom.com, marqoom.sa, marqum.com, thabt.com, thabt.sa, thabat.com, raseen.com, raseen.sa, rseen.com and rasin.com returned no connection (code 000) because of the session's network restrictions. That result does NOT show the domains are unregistered.
- I did not query SAIP (the Saudi Authority for Intellectual Property), WIPO Global Brand Database, LinkedIn, Crunchbase or Google Play search directly. SAIP has no public search I could reach. The only trademark evidence is a Justia (USPTO) web search.
- Because of these limits, every verdict below is provisional: "nothing found" means nothing appeared in about 14 searches, not that the name is cleared.

## 1. إجمال — IJMAL (Ijmal / Ejmal / Ijmaal / Mujmal)

### Takeaway
No software, SaaS, fintech or IT company or app named Ijmal, Ejmal or Ijmaal turned up in Arabic or English searches. The near-matches are in other sectors or differ in spelling. Provisional verdict: **Low risk**.

### Cited Findings
- An English search for "Ijmal app/software/platform" found no exact match. The closest was **Ajmal**, a Muslim well-being and productivity app by "Purpose Pro for Human Development", with $4.99/month and $49/year premium plans — [App Store: Ajmal](https://apps.apple.com/lb/app/ajmal/id6633426653)
- An Arabic search for "منصة إجمال محاسبة" found no accounting platform named إجمال. Results were general accounting tools (Odoo, Accurants, AccountsPortal and others) — [Odoo accounting-firm page](https://www.odoo.com/industries/accounting-firm); [Zoftwarehub Accurants](https://zoftwarehub.com/ar-ae/products/accurants/product-details)
- A search for "Mujmal / Ejmal / Ijmaal app finance" found no app under those names. The nearest was **MyMal**, a personal and small-business finance app focused on Jordan, built by AWT with DSIK, listed in the Finance category — [App Store: MyMal](https://apps.apple.com/ma/app/mymal/id6740701825)
- A Justia (USPTO) trademark search covering IJMAL returned no records — [Justia trademark search results (combined query)](https://trademark.justia.com/987/39/dhamma-98739737.html) (the results page only showed DHAMMA and similar marks; none for IJMAL)

### Inferences
- "Ajmal" (أجمل, "most beautiful") differs from إجمال by one letter in Latin script and sounds close in English. From general knowledge, not checked in this session: Ajmal Perfumes is a large UAE fragrance brand. That is a different sector, so the risk of confusion with an ERP is low, but searches for "Ijmal" may suggest "Ajmal".
- The meaning works well for accounting (الإجمالي = "total"). In English, the "Ij" opening is unusual: speakers may say "idge-mal" or "eye-jmal". "Ijmal" is still the best Latin spelling; "Ejmal" is a secondary form worth defending.
- The brief mentions a "Mujmal" personal-finance app. My searches did NOT confirm it exists, so treat it as unverified until someone checks the stores manually.

### Gaps
- No direct Google Play or App Store search, and no check of ijmal.com or ijmal.sa (network blocked).
- No SAIP or WIPO check.

## 2. ضمّة — DAMMA / DAMMAH (Dhamma, Dama)

### Takeaway
The Arabic form "ضمة" is already the name of a children's educational app on the App Store, and "Dhamma" has a strong religious meaning plus a pending USPTO mark (perfumes). The English sound is also a problem. Provisional verdict: **Medium risk**.

### Cited Findings
- **"ضمة – أنشطة تعليمية للأطفال"** (ضمة – educational activities for children) is on the App Store. It is described as an Arabic/English educational app for early childhood, downloadable in Saudi Arabia and usable offline — [App Store: ضمة](https://apps.apple.com/se/app/%D8%B6%D9%85%D8%A9-%D8%A3%D9%86%D8%B4%D8%B7%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%85%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%84%D9%84%D8%A3%D8%B7%D9%81%D8%A7%D9%84/id1558423272)
- **ضماد (Dimad)** is a Saudi home-nursing app published by WAJHA. It is close in Arabic script, though a different word — [App Store: ضماد](https://apps.apple.com/gw/app/6746129620)
- An English search for "Dammah app Saudi" found nothing named Dammah. Results were about Dammam (the city), e.g. the Dammam Al Ahli Medical Complex app — [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=sa.dnmc)
- USPTO: **DHAMMA PERFUMES**, serial 98739737, filed 9 Sep 2024, Class 3 (perfumes), owner "Abdul Mateen Dhamma Perfumes Industry", status pending (not yet examined) — [Justia](https://trademark.justia.com/987/39/dhamma-98739737.html). Also registered **DHAMA** (reg. 5192143) and **DAMA** (serial 98375400) — [Justia DHAMA](https://trademarks.justia.com/868/10/dhama-86810305.html); [Justia DAMA](https://trademarks.justia.com/983/75/dama-98375400.html)

### Inferences
- Software conflict is low: the ضمة app is a children's education product, not fintech or ERP. But the brief excludes any app with the same name, and this one is in the same Arabic script and available in Saudi Arabia, so the name is not clean.
- English problems: "Damma" is easily read as "damn-a" or "dammit". "Dhamma" is a core Buddhist term (Pali for dharma), so Latin-script searches will be swamped by Buddhist content. "Dama" means "lady" in Spanish and Italian. "Dammam" (a major Saudi city) will dominate Saudi search results and autocomplete.
- In Arabic grammar, "ضمة" is best known as the vowel mark (the "u" diacritic) rather than "gathering together". That weakens the intended meaning for Arabic speakers.

### Gaps
- No Google Play check; could not confirm the ضمة app's developer or whether it is still updated.
- No check of damma.com or dammah.sa; no SAIP or WIPO check.

## 3. مرقوم — MARQOOM / MARQUM / MARQOM

### Takeaway
No exact company, app or trademark named Marqoom, Marqum or Marqom was found. The main risk is **مرقام / Mirqam**, a Saudi Salla-ecosystem analytics and reporting tool from the same Arabic root (ر-ق-م, "number/record"). Provisional verdict: **Low–Medium risk**.

### Cited Findings
- An Arabic search for "مرقوم" with منصة/تطبيق/شركة found no entity using the name. The closest was **"مرقام تسويق (Mirqam)"**, a Salla app that combines store sales, orders and ad performance with exportable reports — [Salla Help Center: مرقام](https://help.salla.sa/article/%D9%85%D8%B1%D9%82%D8%A7%D9%85-%D8%AA%D8%B3%D9%88%D9%8A%D9%82-mirqam/cwdl23hdvdkxtdas9lpu3b4y)
- An English search for "Marqoom app/platform/company" found no match. Similar-sounding results: **Marqby** (legal-tech for trademark registration and IP management) — [Crunchbase: Marqby](https://www.crunchbase.com/organization/marqby); **Marquatica** (a CRM app) — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/marquatica/id6450289168); **Magoom** — [SoftwareSuggest](https://www.softwaresuggest.com/services/magoom)
- A Justia trademark search found no MARQOOM records — [Justia (combined query result set)](https://trademark.justia.com/987/39/dhamma-98739737.html)

### Inferences
- Mirqam (مرقام) and Marqoom (مرقوم) share three of four Arabic root letters, a similar word pattern, and a function close to ERP reporting, inside the Saudi SME/e-commerce ecosystem. That is a real but moderate confusion risk, and a trademark examiner might also note it. The two names are distinct in sound and spelling, though.
- English: "Marqoom" is pronounceable ("mar-KOOM"). The "q" plus "oo" spelling looks unusual, and English speakers will likely write "Marcoom" or "Markoom", so those spellings should be secured as well. The Quranic reference (كتاب مرقوم, "a written record", al-Mutaffifin 83:9 and 83:20) is dignified, but its context is the record of the wicked and the righteous on Judgement Day. Some conservative users may object to using a Quranic phrase commercially. Treat this as a sensitivity to test with users, not a blocker.

### Gaps
- No direct check of marqoom.com, marqoom.sa or marqum.com; no App Store, Google Play, SAIP or WIPO check.

## 4. ثبت — THABT / THABAT / THABIT / SABT

### Takeaway
No company, app or trademark named Thabt or Thabat was found in software, HR or fintech. The weakness is brandability: "ثبّت" is the standard Arabic UI word for "install", and Thabt is hard to pronounce in English. Provisional verdict: **Low risk** for conflicts, but a weak brand.

### Cited Findings
- An English search for "Thabt app Saudi platform" found nothing named Thabt. Results were Tawakkalna, Thriwe, ThriftPlan and similar unrelated apps — [Arab News PK: Tawakkalna](https://www.arabnews.pk/saudi-arabia/tawakkalna-app-expands-daily-digital-services-for-more-than-36-million-users-3003000)
- A search for "Thabat/Thabit attendance HR app Saudi ثبات" found nothing under those names. Saudi HR and attendance apps found: Mawqoot, Basmtak HR, Jisr, Mojiz, Bench HR — [App Store: Mawqoot](https://apps.apple.com/cz/app/mawqoot/id1621890777); [Jisr attendance](https://www.jisr.net/en/attendance-management)
- An Arabic search for "ثبت" with منصة/تطبيق/شركة تقنية returned only unrelated tech apps (Techen, تقنية CS, Thiqati, منصة ضمان). None was named ثبت — [App Store: Thiqati](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/thiqati/id6468778547); [App Store: منصة ضمان](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/%D9%85%D9%86%D8%B5%D8%A9-%D8%B6%D9%85%D8%A7%D9%86/id1590697442)

### Inferences
- The noisy Arabic results show the problem: ثبت/ثبّت is a very common verb ("install", "fix", "pin", "confirm"), so a brand named ثبت will be very hard to find in Arabic search and app stores. The phrase "ثبّت التطبيق" ("install the app") will collide with the brand.
- English: "Thabt" ends in a three-consonant cluster with no vowel ("th-b-t"), which is awkward. "Thabat" is easier to say but means "ثبات" (steadiness), a different word. "Thabit" is a common Arabic personal name. "Sabt" (how Levantine and Egyptian speakers often say it) also means "Saturday" (السبت).
- With no visible users, legal conflict risk is low, but SEO and pronunciation costs are high.

### Gaps
- Not checked: domains thabt.com, thabt.sa and thabat.com; Google Play; and SAIP, WIPO or USPTO for "THABAT/THABIT" in software classes (Justia was not searched for these spellings).

## 5. رصين — RASEEN / RASIN / RAZEEN / RSEEN

### Takeaway
The exact Arabic name is already used by a live App Store app, **"رصين | Rseen"**, and the Latin "Ras-" space is crowded with Saudi and GCC fintech and accounting brands (Rasan, Raseed/Rasseed). Provisional verdict: **High risk**. It fails the brief's "not used by any app" rule.

### Cited Findings
- **"رصين | Rseen"** (App Store id6739260369) is a marketplace for construction and interior-decoration services that connects users with engineering offices and contractors. It is free, listed under Graphics & Design, and its seller is Nawaf Alabdulwahed — [App Store: رصين | Rseen](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6739260369)
- **Rasan (رسن)**, a Saudi fintech, is on Forbes Middle East's "Fintech 50 2026" list — [Forbes ME: Rasan](https://www.forbesmiddleeast.com/ar/lists/the-middle-easts-fintech-50-2026/rasan/). There is also a separate "rasan رسن" services app — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6478466574)
- **Rasseed**, founded in Saudi Arabia in 2016, makes gift-card purchasing software and was acquired by Saudi q-commerce company Nana — [Wamda](https://www.wamda.com/en/2024/10/saudi-q-commerce-nana-acquires-egyptian-fintech-raseedi)
- **Raseed** is cloud billing, POS, inventory and accounting software for SMEs (India/GST-focused) — [GetApp: Raseed](https://www.getapp.com/finance-accounting-software/a/raseed/); [Capterra: Raseed](https://www.capterra.ca/software/1073647/Raseed). **Raseed Invest** is a GCC investing app — [raseedinvest.com](https://raseedinvest.com/support/getting-started/about-raseed/what-is-raseed-app)
- Other Saudi apps with similar names: **Raseel Gifts** — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1559507627); **منصة رصد (Raa9d)**, a trading-analysis app — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/sa/app/%D9%85%D9%86%D8%B5%D8%A9-%D8%B1%D8%B5%D8%AF-raa9d-platform/id6736729089?l=ar)
- No company named "Razeen" turned up in fintech, accounting or ERP searches (the results were all Raseed) — [Slashdot: Raseed](https://slashdot.org/software/p/Raseed/)
- A Justia trademark search found no RASEEN records — [Justia (combined query result set)](https://trademark.justia.com/987/39/dhamma-98739737.html)

### Inferences
- An identical Arabic name on a live Saudi-developed app (the seller's name is Gulf Arabic) is a direct conflict in Saudi app stores, even though its sector (construction services) differs.
- "Raseen" sounds and looks like Raseed and Rasseed, which are accounting, POS and fintech brands in Saudi Arabia and abroad. That confusion lands right in the target category.
- English: "Raseen" is easy to say, but sounds like "raisin" (and "Rasin" is spelled almost the same), which invites jokes.

### Gaps
- Not checked: whether the Rseen app is still active or has a registered company or trademark behind it; raseen.com and raseen.sa; Google Play; SAIP or WIPO.

## Overall ranking and recommended next steps (inference)
- Ranking by conflict risk, lowest first: **IJMAL ≈ THABT < MARQOOM < DAMMA < RASEEN**. Weighing brandability as well, **IJMAL** and **MARQOOM** are the strongest. THABT has low conflict risk but weak findability and pronunciation. DAMMA has pronunciation and meaning problems. Drop RASEEN.
- Before committing, do these manual checks that this session could not do:
  1. Search the SAIP trademark registry in Classes 9, 35, 36 and 42 for the Arabic and Latin forms.
  2. Search WIPO Global Brand Database for the same.
  3. Search the App Store and Google Play in the Saudi storefronts.
  4. Run WHOIS on .com, .sa and .com.sa for every spelling.
  5. Search LinkedIn and Crunchbase company names.
  6. Verify whether a "Mujmal" app exists, because it was not confirmed here.
