## Q1. Which names already exist in this market (the "names to avoid" map)?

### Takeaway
The Saudi SME accounting, ERP and POS space is crowded with short Arabic common nouns taken from the bookkeeping vocabulary: قيود, دفترة, دفاتر, سند, ميزان/Mezan, فاتورة/Fatora, الأمين, المحاسب. Two other fields are also crowded: virtue and quality words (وافق, مرن, دقة, شمول, الوجيز) and weights and measures (ميزان, مكيال). شمول ("comprehensiveness") is already an ERP brand and مكيال (a measure) is already a Riyadh fintech. Both bear directly on the semantic fields under consideration: "total/comprehensive" and measure words such as مثقال.

### Inferences
- **Crowded semantic fields:**
  - **Bookkeeping nouns:** قيود، دفترة، دفاتر، سند، ميزان، فاتورة، الأستاذ، المحاسب، الكاشير. Every short accounting term is taken or nearly so. A new name in this field will be weak and close to existing marks.
  - **Quality/virtue words:** وافق، مرن، دقة، شمول، الوجيز، صحيح، الأمين، رواء.
  - **Measures:** ميزان، مكيال, plus مثقال under consideration. This field holds Mezan and Mekyal (fintech, Class 36 risk). مثقال is phonetically distinct from both, but it is in the same conceptual field.
  - **"Comprehensive/total":** شمول (Shumoul ERP) is already taken. شامل/متكامل/كامل are all near it conceptually, and المتكامل is a common descriptor in POS marketing copy.
- **Less crowded:** invented or coined Arabic words, rare classical words with no software meaning, place, nature or heritage words unrelated to accounting, and Latin-coined English names.
- **Generic tokens to avoid** because they are both common and descriptive: "ERP", "Cloud/سحابي", "Fatoora/فاتورة", "Hesab/حساب", "Daftar/دفتر", "Mizan/ميزان".

## Q2. What makes a name registrable and protectable in Saudi Arabia (trademark law, distinctiveness, similarity, classes, procedure, cost, time)?

### Takeaway
Saudi Arabia applies the GCC Trademark Law, implemented by SAIP. It is **first-to-file**: prior use gives little protection. Signs that lack distinctiveness, describe the goods or services, or are their customary names are excluded. Several practitioners say Saudi practice does **not** accept acquired distinctiveness for descriptive marks. Official fees are about **SAR 6,500–7,500 per class (≈ USD 1,700–2,000)**, plus agent fees. A clean application takes roughly **5–12 months**. Saudi Arabia joined the **Madrid Protocol on 8 Oct 2026**.

### Cited Findings
**Legal basis / non-registrable signs**
- The GCC Trademark Law defines a mark as anything with a distinctive form (names, words, logos, etc.). Its exclusion list (Art. 3 in the GCC text) includes signs lacking distinctive character. In Saudi Arabia's official translation, the exclusion list sits in Article 2. Its first item covers signs devoid of distinctive features, signs describing product/service characteristics, and common names customarily given to the products or services. — [GCC Trademark Law (JPO copy)](https://www.jpo.go.jp/e/system/laws/gaikoku/document/index/gcc-e_trademark-law.pdf); [Saudi Law of Trademarks – BOE translation (misa.gov.sa)](https://misa.gov.sa/app/uploads/2025/08/Law-of-Trademarks.pdf); [SAIP-hosted GCC law (EN)](https://externalportal-backend-production.saip.gov.sa/sites/default/files/2022-06/%D9%82%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%88%D9%86%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%AA%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%AC%D8%A7%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A9%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AE%D9%84%D9%8A%D8%AC%D9%8A%20%D8%A8%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%86%D8%AC%D9%84%D9%8A%D8%B2%D9%8A%D8%A9.pdf)
- Other exclusions:
  - marks identical or similar to marks famous in the Kingdom (well-known marks)
  - marks contrary to religion or resembling religious symbols
  - misleading data
  
  — [Saudi translation via search summary](https://misa.gov.sa/app/uploads/2025/08/Law-of-Trademarks.pdf)
- Practitioner sources say generic or descriptive marks are not accepted. They also say that, unlike some countries, a descriptive mark cannot be registered in Saudi Arabia on the strength of long use. This is a secondary-source claim. — [Abu Najm (abounaja)](https://abounaja.com/blog/trademark-registration-in-saudi-arabia); [Kadasa](https://kadasa.com.sa/understanding-trademark-rights-for-saudi-companies/); [Lexology (older)](https://www.lexology.com/library/detail.aspx?g=214d2ca0-9e1d-40d2-8ce3-517a27ee8d9b)
- SAIP examiners compare marks visually and phonetically, in Arabic and other languages. A mark that looks distinctive in English may become descriptive or offensive when transliterated into Arabic. Similarity in pronunciation, meaning, Arabic translation or overall impression can each ground a refusal or an opposition. — [IGBS 2026 guide](https://ig-bs.com/trademark-registration-saudi-arabia/); [Effemark SAIP search guide](https://www.effemark.com/articles/saudi-arabia-trademark-search-saip-registry-guide-brand-protection)

**Classes**
- Saudi Arabia uses the Nice Classification, 12th edition, across all 45 classes. — [IGBS](https://ig-bs.com/trademark-registration-saudi-arabia/); [search summary, analytix](https://analytix.sa/how-to-register-a-trademark-in-saudi-arabia/)
- Class 9 covers downloadable or installed software. Class 42 covers SaaS and cloud software provided as a service. Specifications should state the software's function and field. This guidance is USPTO/general practice used as an analogy, not SAIP guidance. — [WikiTrademarks](https://wikitrademarks.com/updates/class-9-vs-class-42-software-saas-trademark); [Webx](https://www.webx.net.tr/en/blog/trademark-class-for-software); [JCA UAE](https://jcatrademarkuae.com/a-guide-trademark-classes-relevant-to-software-brands/)

**First-to-file / prior use**
- Rights go to the first to register, even against an earlier user, including someone who has traded under the name abroad. — [Perspire IP](https://www.perspireip.com/blog/trademark-registration-in-saudi-arabia/); [Kawkaba](https://kawkaba.sa/en/insights/trademark-registration-saudi); [ATB Legal](https://atblegal.com/blog/intellectual-property-laww/trademark-opposition-cancellation/)

**Publication / opposition / term**
- Accepted marks are published on SAIP's site. The opposition period is **60 days** from publication (90 days before the 2016 amendments). One source describes a 30-day window and a 60-day applicant reply, so sources conflict on procedural periods. — [Lexology at a glance](https://www.lexology.com/library/detail.aspx?g=80bb3fdc-d1f1-48fe-9fe2-696151e9e2a4); [ATB Legal](https://atblegal.com/blog/intellectual-property-laww/trademark-opposition-cancellation/)
- Protection lasts **10 Hijri years** from the filing date (≈ 9 years 8 months). Renewal is filed in the final year, with a 6-month grace period. — [Audit Firm Online 2026](https://auditfirmonline.com/trademark-registration-in-saudi-arabia/); [ip-coster](https://ip-coster.com/IPGuides/trademark-saudi-arabia)
- Article numbers for publication (Art. 14 vs Art. 16) conflict across secondary sources. — [ATB Legal](https://atblegal.com/blog/intellectual-property-laww/trademark-opposition-cancellation/)

### Inferences
- **On a common Arabic dictionary word for "total/comprehensive" (شامل، كامل، إجمالي، متكامل) for software in Classes 9/42:**
  - Such a word describes a quality of the service ("comprehensive ERP"). It is the kind of sign the Article 2 exclusion targets: devoid of distinctiveness or describing characteristics.
  - Because acquired distinctiveness reportedly is not accepted, a refusal would be hard to overcome. Expect refusal of the word alone, or registration only as a composite logo with a disclaimer, which gives weak and narrow protection.
  - It would also sit next to the existing شمول Shumoul ERP brand.
- **On a dictionary word with no direct meaning for the services (e.g. a measure word like مثقال, or an unrelated classical noun):**
  - It is suggestive or arbitrary rather than descriptive, and is more likely registrable.
  - The risk shifts to similarity with ميزان/مكيال-type marks in Classes 9/36/42.
- **Recommended filing:**
  - Classes 9 and 42 at minimum.
  - Add 35 for business/accounting management services and possibly POS retail services.
  - Add 36 if payments, financing or wallet features are planned, since adjacent fintechs (Mekyal, Hala) would make 36 the opposition-risk class.
  - File both the Arabic word mark and the Latin transliteration, separately or as a combined mark, because SAIP compares across scripts.
  - At ~SAR 6,500–7,500 per class, two classes cost ≈ SAR 13–15k official, and four classes ≈ SAR 26–30k, before agent fees.
- **Timing:** first-to-file makes it important to file before public launch or announcement.

## Q3. Must the commercial-register trade name be Arabic, and how does it interact with an English brand?

### Takeaway
No longer strictly. Under the new **Trade Names Law (effective 3 April 2025)**, a trade name may be Arabic, Arabized, or English words, letters or numbers. Reservation costs **SAR 200 (Arabic) vs SAR 500 (English)**, and the state actively promotes Arabic names. The trade name (MoC) and the trademark (SAIP) are separate regimes, so the brand still needs SAIP registration to be protected as a mark.

### Cited Findings
- The Trade Name Law and its implementing regulations took effect **3 April 2025**. — [Gibson Dunn (via search)](https://www.gibsondunn.com/saudi-arabias-new-trade-name-law-legal-update/); [Clyde & Co Sept 2025](https://www.clydeco.com/en/insights/2025/09/ksa-commercial-registration-law-and-trade-name-law); [DWF Apr 2025](https://dwfgroup.com/ar-ae/news-and-insights/insights/2025/4/saudi-commercial-register-law-and-law-of-trade-names)
- The Ministry of Commerce's July 2025 guidance accepts Arabic or Arabized words, Arabic letters or numbers, or a combination, and also English words, letters or numbers. Names must not violate public order or morals, cause confusion, or be similar to an already reserved or registered name. — [MoC news 27-07-25](https://mc.gov.sa/en/mediacenter/News/Pages/27-07-25-03.aspx)
- Clyde & Co says English names need an accompanying Arabic transliteration and are assessed by their Arabic equivalents. This conflicts with, or adds to, the MoC summary. — [Clyde & Co](https://www.clydeco.com/en/insights/2025/09/ksa-commercial-registration-law-and-trade-name-law)
- Fees: SAR 200 to reserve an Arabic name and SAR 500 for an English name. — [Gibson Dunn (via search)](https://www.gibsondunn.com/saudi-arabias-new-trade-name-law-legal-update/)
- Names using "Saudi", city or region names, or family names face special conditions. Religious, military and political references are barred. — [DWF](https://dwfgroup.com/ar-ae/news-and-insights/insights/2025/4/saudi-commercial-register-law-and-law-of-trade-names); [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/world/gulf/saudi/saudi-arabia-sr50000-fine-for-new-trade-name-rule-violations-1.104308076)
- The law created an Arabic Language Experts Platform, and MoC is directed to promote Arabic names. — [Saudi Gazette](https://saudigazette.com.sa/article/650637); [DWF](https://dwfgroup.com/ar-ae/news-and-insights/insights/2025/4/saudi-commercial-register-law-and-law-of-trade-names)
- Penalties: up to SAR 50,000 (Gulf News), while Clyde & Co cites SAR 1,000–10,000 for certain violations. The figures may refer to different violations. — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/world/gulf/saudi/saudi-arabia-sr50000-fine-for-new-trade-name-rule-violations-1.104308076); [Clyde & Co](https://www.clydeco.com/en/insights/2025/09/ksa-commercial-registration-law-and-trade-name-law)
- Official English text: [Law of Tradenames (misa.gov.sa)](https://misa.gov.sa/app/uploads/2025/08/Law-of-Tradenames.pdf). Not readable in this session.

### Inferences
- The cleanest structure is an Arabic trade name that matches the Arabic brand: cheaper (SAR 200), aligned with the policy favouring Arabic, and consistent for .com.sa eligibility.
- The English transliteration is then protected by SAIP trademark filings, not by the trade name.
- If an English trade name is chosen, expect it to be examined through its Arabic rendering. A transliteration that collides in Arabic with an existing reserved name could be refused even if the Latin spelling differs.
- A reserved trade name does not by itself give trademark rights, and a trademark does not reserve the trade name. Both should be secured close together.

### Gaps
- The exact Arabic text of the Trade Names Law and its regulations was not read, so these remain unverified: the transliteration requirement, the reservation validity period, and whether a trade name may conflict with a registered SAIP trademark (often a ground for refusal).

## Q4. Step-by-step clearance checklist and approximate cost/time

### Takeaway
Clear the name in this order: market and name screen, then SAIP search across scripts and classes 9/35/42/36, then WIPO Global Brand Database and GCC offices, then MoC trade-name availability, then .sa/.com and social handles. File in KSA in classes 9 and 42 (plus 35, and 36 if relevant) **before** launch. Budget ≈ SAR 13–15k official for two classes, and about 5–12 months to registration.

### Cited Findings
- SAIP registry search is the first clearance step. Similarity is judged on pronunciation, meaning, Arabic translation and overall impression. — [Effemark SAIP search guide](https://www.effemark.com/articles/saudi-arabia-trademark-search-saip-registry-guide-brand-protection)
- Madrid now covers KSA as of 8 Oct 2026, so the WIPO Madrid Monitor and Global Brand Database become relevant for designations into KSA. Five GCC states are in Madrid. — [WIPO](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/madrid-system/w/news/2026/saudi-arabia-joins-wipos-madrid-system?lang-wt=ar); [Asia IP Law](https://asiaiplaw.com/section/cover-story/saudi-arabia-joins-madrid-system-as-gulf-trademark-landscape-shifts)
- **.sa domains (SaudiNIC):**
  - .com.sa requires a valid Saudi Commercial Registration or a locally registered trade name. The domain should match the name or trademark or be closely related to the activity. If it does not match the CR name, a Saudi trademark is required.
  - Plain .sa is open more broadly.
  - Disputes go to SaudiNIC mediation first, then WIPO. The .sa policy also covers trade names.
  
  — [SaudiNIC regulation](https://nic.sa/en/help/regulation); [Agip](https://www.agip.com/Agip-Country-Service/en/120/DN/2); [Gandi](https://www.gandi.net/en/tlds/sa); [WIPO ccTLD SA](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/amc/domain-name-disputes/cctld/sa)
- Timeline: about 5 months ([iGERENT](https://igerent.com/trademark-registration-saudi-arabia)) to 6–12 months ([DIAC](https://diac.ae/saudi-arabia/trademark-registration/)), plus a 60-day opposition window ([Lexology](https://www.lexology.com/library/detail.aspx?g=80bb3fdc-d1f1-48fe-9fe2-696151e9e2a4)).
- Trade-name reservation costs SAR 200 Arabic or SAR 500 English. — [Gibson Dunn (via search)](https://www.gibsondunn.com/saudi-arabias-new-trade-name-law-legal-update/)

### Inferences — proposed checklist (synthesised from the sources above)
1. **Semantic screen.** Reject candidates that are descriptive, generic or laudatory for accounting/ERP: شامل، متكامل، إجمالي، حساب، فاتورة، دفتر، ميزان، قيد، سند. Also reject government platform names (فاتورة، مدد، قوى). Check Arabic meaning, dialect connotations and the transliteration's meaning in English.
2. **Market screen.** Check the candidate against the Q1 map, both scripts, phonetically: Qoyod, Wafeq, Daftra, Dafater, SMACC, Snad/Sanad, Shumoul, Mezan, Mekyal, Foodics, Rewaa, Marn, Jisr, Hala, Moyasar and others. Run App Store and Google Play searches in Arabic and English.
3. **SAIP search.** Search exact, phonetic and translated variants in classes 9, 42, 35 and 36. Note that SAIP compares across Arabic and Latin scripts.
4. **WIPO Global Brand Database and Madrid Monitor.** Check designations covering SA and the GCC (UAE, Oman, Bahrain and others), because Madrid filings can now reach KSA with an 18-month refusal window.
5. **GCC national offices** where expansion is planned: UAE MoE, Kuwait, Qatar, Bahrain, Oman.
6. **MoC trade-name availability check** through the Saudi Business Center / MoC e-services. Reserve the Arabic name (SAR 200).
7. **Domains and handles.** Check .sa/.com.sa via SaudiNIC WHOIS (.com.sa needs CR or trademark), .com, and X/Instagram/TikTok/LinkedIn/Snapchat handles in both scripts.
8. **File SAIP applications** before public launch (first-to-file). File the Arabic word mark and the Latin word mark, in classes 9 and 42 plus 35, and 36 if payments or financing are planned. Draft specifications naming the software functions: accounting, e-invoicing, POS, inventory, payroll/HR.
9. **Budget and timeline.**
   - ≈ SAR 6,500–7,500 official per class per mark, plus agent fees (not found).
   - Two classes × two marks (Arabic + Latin) ≈ SAR 26–30k official.
   - A combined Arabic/Latin mark halves the official cost but narrows flexibility.
   - About 5–12 months to certificate.
   - Monitor the SAIP gazette for 60 days after publication, and watch for later conflicting filings.
